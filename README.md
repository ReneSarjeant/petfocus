# Pet Focus Bar

Pet Focus Bar adds a movable orange bar that shows your hunter pet's current Focus in World of Warcraft: Forever. The bar appears below the center of the screen and hides when you have no pet summoned.

## Installation

1. Copy the `PetFocusBar` folder into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.
2. Restart WoW and enable **Pet Focus Bar** in the AddOns menu.

Hold **Shift** and **left-drag** the bar to move it. Its position is saved per character.
